About me

Engineer. Writer. Perpetually curious.

I'm a Software Engineer at ArthaVedh Consulting, where I ship enterprise platforms in Python, Docker, and PostgreSQL. My work sits at the intersection of backend engineering and DevOps — designing service boundaries, running API gateways, and making infrastructure invisible to the developers who depend on it.

Before that, I spent a year there as an intern (Mar–Sep 2024), got my first production exposure to cloud infrastructure, and learned how to write Go that doesn't embarrass me a week later. Earlier experience came from LiNS (Spring Boot, JDBC), ONE IT Solutions (client-facing web work), and IEEE Bombay Section (volunteer full-stack).

Right now, I'm going deep on generative and agentic AI — not just using the APIs, but understanding how agents plan, use tools, and recover from failure. The next generation of software will be AI-native, and I intend to build parts of it.
