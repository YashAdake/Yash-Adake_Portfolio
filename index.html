About Me

Engineer. Problem-solver. Lifelong learner.

Computer Science graduate turned backend and DevOps engineer — obsessed with building systems that just work.

I'm a Software Engineer at ArthaVedh Consulting Pvt Ltd, where I work with Python, Docker, and PostgreSQL to ship enterprise-grade platforms. My focus is on reliability, observability, and making infrastructure invisible to the developers who depend on it.

I gravitate toward cloud infrastructure and automation — there's a quiet satisfaction in watching a deployment pipeline run green, or shaving seconds off a cold start. I've shipped production workloads on CloudJiffy, documented API surfaces with Swagger, and wrangled data pipelines with Apache NiFi.

Right now, I'm going deep on Generative AI and Agentic AI — not just using the APIs, but understanding how agents plan, tool-use, and recover from failure. The next generation of software will be AI-native, and I plan to build parts of it.

If you're working on something ambitious in backend, DevOps, or applied AI — I'd love to hear about it.
